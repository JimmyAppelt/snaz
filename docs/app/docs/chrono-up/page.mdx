# Chrono Up

## Available format codes

Code | Outputs
------------ | -------------
`$d` | days
`$h` | hours
`$m` | minutes 
`$s` | seconds
`$totalminutes` | total minutes

## Format examples

Example format | Live output
------------ | -------------
`$h`:`$m` | 10:15 
`$h`:`$m`:`$s` | 10:15:35
`$d` days and `$h`:`$m`:`$s` | 2 days and 10:15:35
`$d` days `$h` hours `$m` minutes `$s` seconds | 2 days 10 hours 15 minutes 35 seconds
`$totalminutes`:`$s` | 75:35 *(e.g. 1:15:35 will be 75 minutes)*
`$h` foo `$m` minutes `$s` seconds| 10 foo 15 minutes 35 seconds
